# 4 Months After My "Hello World" Post

## It's Been a While!

So it's been 4 months since my first post and unexpectedly, there are some changes in my life or should I say, in my CTF life and college life. In this post, I want to share about it.

## CTF Life

I'm still actively playing CTFs every weekend with TCP1P, and in September, we got 3rd place globally in 07CTF 2026 by 0bscuri7y! Just like I mentioned in my first post, I also joined several local CTFs in Indonesia such as Hology 9.0 by Brawijaya University, TryHards CTF by TryHards Community, Polri CTF by E-Sport Kapolri Cup, Cyber Frontier by Siber Sentinel Asia and Semarang City Government, and of course, Gemastik 19 by the Ministry of Higher Education, Science, and Technology (which held its finals at Binus University). Well, I haven't qualified for the finals in any of those competitions yet, tho.

![07CTF 2026 TCP1P 3rd Place](/blog-images/2/07ctf.png)

During those 4 months, I also got 2 CVEs, CVE-2026-13332 (9.1 by CISA-ADP) and CVE-2026-93779 (6.5 by Wordfence), which was also my first paid bounty! It felt so good to earn money by yourself while in college. I also switched my niche in CTF from Forensics to OSINT (Open Source Intelligence). Actually, I didn't completely leave Forensics, I just shifted my focus. Now I play CTFs with OSINT as my primary category and Forensics as my secondary category. Why? Idk, I just feel like I enjoy CTFs more when I play OSINT instead of Forensics. And I also love to shock my friends with my OSINT skills, especially GEOINT hahah.

That's all for my CTF life. Let's see what's up with my college life!

## College Life

Umm, not much has changed in my college life, actually. I'm currently in my 5th semester (in Indonesia, college students normally spend 8 semesters to graduate). Right now, while writing this blog, I'm in my finals week for this semester. After this, I'll be entering my 6th semester, only a year and a half left until graduation (if I graduate on time)! I'm also starting a 1-month internship from December 21st to January 21st.

## Moving Forward

So that's all for my CTF and college life update. For the next few months, I'll try to survive this finals week, wrap up my internship, and level up my OSINT skills. My dream in CTF is that I really want to qualify for an on-site final at least once, whether it's national or international (I also love reading other teams' stories about attending international finals like The SAS CON CTF Final). Well, that's all, I think. See you in my next post a few months from now!
