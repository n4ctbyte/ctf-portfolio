## Hello World!

Okay so, hi there! I don't know how you found this blog but enjoy! This is my first post on this blog so let's get started! Actually idk what to write for my first post and suddenly, I thought of something. Why not make like a "hello world" for my blog? So that is where this post comes from. I want to talk about myself, how I got into cybersecurity, and why I started writing a blog.

## How I stumbled into Cybersecurity

Let's move back a bit further. Everything started from back then when I was in junior high school third year, going to senior high school. When I was in my last year of junior high in 2020, as we all know, COVID-19 came and the whole world got into a massive lockdown. School was held online, workers worked from home, the streets were empty, and the city felt like a dead city. I got bored during that time. Especially since we 3rd year students get our break earlier. I played games and scrolled through social media all day, every day. One day, when I was looking for a Youtube video to watch, I stumbled into a video from "Web Programming Unpas" channel video talking about a roadmap to get into web development in 2021.

![WPU Roadmap Web Dev](/blog-images/wpu-roadmap.png)

Because it looks interesting, I clicked the video and watched it till the end of the video. Then, I feel like this is it. "This is interesting!", I thought. From there, I learn web development (I already have basic coding from my junior high). I learnt HTML, CSS, Flexbox, CSS Grid, and built my first portfolio website (well, more like a copy from a youtube video haha). After that, I learnt about JavaScript. But idk why, it just felt like I don't like this thing. It's not like super hard, it just like not my passion. I searched for other paths in IT. And from there I know that there are so many paths in IT. Web dev, game dev, AI, cybersecurity, and many more. Doing a little research, I feel interested in AI development. So I learnt about AI and bot. Long story short, I built my first project in this field which is a Discord bot named "Yuuki" (I got this name from Yuuki in SAO, my favorite character). At first, it wasn't even an AI. It was just a bot with static instructions and features such as task reminder, music player, and youtube video downloader. A few months later, I gave it a chatbot feature with Llama API. After that, I got into university, Riau University. In my first term of my first year in university, I took an online class from Dicoding about AI and machine learning. I finished the first class which is a Python Programming class. It was easy because I was used to Python from building Yuuki. After that class, there was a machine learning class. I didn't really understand some parts of it because it is so math-heavy and I didn't finish that class. Because of that, I got into this burnout and identity crisis phase. I don't see how I would work in AI industry. I don't enjoy learning about machine learning. It is so boring and math-heavy. I'm a practical person. I love learning something by really doing it, not just theoretical. That is why I built Yuuki back then. And sadly, I felt like AI/ML was more theoretical than practical. After that, a few months later I stopped learning AI/ML, but was still developing Yuuki and gave it many new features such as task reminder, 8ball, and free games reminder from Epic Games and Steam.

![Yuuki Discord Bot](/blog-images/yuuki-bot.png)

One day, I watched a hacking movie, "Who Am I". It looks so cool. Hacking, taking down systems, breaking into systems without being known. And I tried learning cybersecurity. I watched this 12-hours long Youtube video about cybersecurity basics and Kali Linux VM setup. After that, I started doing CTF on picoCTF for a few months. I do the red-team-ish category such as web exploitation. After finishing up all the easy challenges in web exploitation, I moved to other categories and found forensics category. And I found it so much fun and interesting. From there, I kept doing all forensics categories in picoCTF and started doing cryptography.

![WHO AM I Movie](/blog-images/who-am-i.png)

Long story short, I joined my first CTF event individually which is picoCTF 2026. There, I wiped out all forensics, cryptography, and general skills challenges. After that, I joined a Discord community, Vincoo, because I wanted to participate in the ARG challenge and test my forensic and OSINT skills. One day, a CTF community named "TCP1P" made a collaboration with Vincoo to do VincooCTF. I then joined TCP1P community's Discord server and played my first team CTF with them, RITSEC CTF 2026 where we got the 3rd place globally. From there, I always joined CTF every week with TCP1P and enhanced my skills in forensics, cryptography, and OSINT until today and into the future. Btw, we achieved many things such as 3rd place in RITSEC CTF 2026, 11th place in BYUCTF 2026, 18th place with full solve in BroncoCTF 2026, and many 2-digits ranks in other CTFs.

![RITSEC CTF 2026 3rd Place](/blog-images/ctftime-ritsec.png)

## Why I Started Writing

Now that I've shared my background and why I got into cybersecurity, I want to share about my reason to start writing a blog. I love telling people about what I like and what I'm passionate about. Whether it's a project I'm working on, a scientific phenomenon I just learnt, a really good movie or anime I just watched, an update in tech, literally anything I found interesting. I also wanted to enhance my writing skills outside of writing a writeup for CTFs. And because of that, I made a page in my portfolio website to share about all of that. So this is like my "digital garden" where I grow ideas, share about my current projects, my campus life, myself, anything. So don't expect my posts to be all about tech and CTF.

## My Goals in the Future

In the future, I picture myself working in DFIR (Digital Forensics and Incident Response) while still participating in CTFs. For the near future, this year, I want to join Gemastik 19 and other local CTFs in Indonesia. Next year, I want to join CBC (Cyber Breaker Competition) by Team RRQ x Peris.AI Season 4. And many more goals will come. Thanks for reading this long story about myself in my first post and see you in the next post!
